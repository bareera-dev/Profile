Admission FAQS


When do applications open?

Applications open in November. Please check the website in November for an announcement.


When is the best time to apply?

As early as possible. Admissions are on a rolling basis, hence those who apply early will have better chance of securing a seat at FPS. We tend to conduct majority of our interviews by November, December and January. Limited seats are available after.


What is the addmissions citeria?

Admissions are solely based on merit. Selection is based on a holistic review of the entire application.


What is the acceptance rate?

We do not reveal the specific acceptance rate, but admissions at FPS are extremely competitive..


Is addmission open for all grafe levels?

Admissions are only open from Pre-Nursery to Grade 9. We do not accept any applications for Grade X and XI.


Are siblings guaranteed admission?

No. Siblings are given a degree of preference, but admissions are contingent on performance in the interview and test.


Is there testing and interviewing for all grades?

There is no testing for Pre-Nursery applicants.

  • For Nursery and Kindergarten students, testing and interview are held on the same day.
  • For Grade 1 – Grade 9: Interviews are conducted first, and then a test is conducted at a later date for those.
    • students that are deemed to be a good fit for the school.


I applied over 2 weeks ago but I have not received a response?

We receive a high volume of applications each year, and we aim to respond to applications as quickly as possible in the order that they were received. If you have applied after the month of February, please expect a response within 4 weeks.


Can I reschedule the interview/test?

No. Reschedules are only possible in extenuating circumstances. We cannot guarantee another interview / test date due to the high volume of applications.


Can I apply multiple times?

No. If you have not received admission this year, then you must wait and apply for the next academic year.


Is there an application fee?

Yes. There is a RS. 1500 application fee that must be submitted at the time of interview.
