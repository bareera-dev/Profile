Overveiw

The curriculum at Foundation Public School focuses on a rigorous and creative academic foundation that aims at developing intellectual curiosity, critical thinking, and problem-solving skills amongst our students.

Our renowned curriculum and devoted faculty, whose tenure averages 10 years, uphold the high standards we have maintained for nearly four decades.

We also ensure to maintain an ardent connection with the parent community and keep them abreast with their child's progress at each stage.

No matter what career students ultimately pursue, they graduate FPS with the knowledge, skills and confidence to make a lasting impact on this world.

Our academic program is organized around three schools:




 Elementary School

   At FPS Elementary, we nurture the development of each child emotionally, academically, physically, socially, and artistically during their formative years.




Junior School

At FPS, we consider Junior schools as an opportunity for students to explore and develop their cognitive, social, and physical skills.




Senior School

FPS Senior schools strike a perfect harmony between a rigorous curriculum and an active co-curricular program. The aim of the senior school is to prepare our students for A Level, university and beyond.
